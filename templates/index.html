📚 Multi-Reference Comparison & Validation

🤖 AI wall check (Gemini) - automatic

After every scan the AI works in two stages: (1) it reviews the whole plan in zoomed views to find wrong and missing walls, (2) it looks at a close-up image of every single wall and classifies it (wall / column / door swing / window / furniture / stair / artifact). A piece is removed only when the signals agree; missed walls are added only when they snap onto real CAD wall lines. Every added wall is snapped exactly onto real CAD wall lines; clean wall rectangles from the CAD are protected and never deleted. The Gemini key is built in - nothing to paste.
